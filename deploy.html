Deploy your team.

One screen, every channel, with the true status of each. Nothing is switched on until you switch it on.

Your account

Connect your tools

Send what your team learns to the tools you already use. Test conversations are never sent.

Booking

Paste your booking page from Calendly, Cal.com, Acuity, Square, OpenTable, Resy, Vagaro, Booksy, Jobber or any other tool. Every agent gives it to customers who want to book, and your website chat shows a Book a time button.

HubSpot

When a customer leaves an email or phone number, Squadron adds or updates the HubSpot contact and attaches the message and chat as a note. In HubSpot, open Settings, Integrations, Private Apps, create an app with the contacts read and write scopes, and paste its access token here.

Any other CRM, by webhook

Squadron posts each event as JSON to your address, signed so you can check it came from Squadron. Use a Zapier or Make catch hook to reach Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, GoHighLevel, Google Sheets, Slack or anything else. Events: a customer asked for a person, the team took a message, a phone call ended, and a question the team could not answer.

The human layer

Choose how your AI team and your people share the work. Every channel follows this choice.

On-call and hours

When a customer asks for a person, is upset, or hits something the profile does not cover, the team transfers or takes a message for the on-call contact.
