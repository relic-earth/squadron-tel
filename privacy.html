Privacy Policy.

Effective September 23, 2026.

This policy explains what Squadron collects, why, and who helps us process it. Squadron is operated by Island Global Co, doing business as Squadron (“Squadron”, “we”). This policy covers two groups of people: businesses that use Squadron, and the customers who talk to those businesses' Squadron agents.

1. What we collect from businesses

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Business content: the public pages of the website or App Store listing you give us, the documents you upload, and the business profile and agents built from them, including your edits.
  • Billing records: your plan, invoices and payment status. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and never reach our servers. Bank wires are matched by their reference through our bank, Mercury.
  • Settings and usage: your channel settings, phone numbers and the minutes used.

2. What we collect from callers and chat visitors

  • Chat: the messages exchanged with the agent.
  • Phone: the caller's phone number, the call time and length, an audio recording of the call and a written transcript. Every call begins with a notice that it is answered by an AI and recorded.
  • Messages left for the business: any name, number or request the caller gives so that a person can follow up.

We process this information on behalf of the business the customer contacted. That business decides what happens to it and is the right first contact for requests about it.

3. How we use it

  • To answer customers for the business, and to show the business its conversations, recordings and gaps in Squadron HQ.
  • To bill, to enforce prepaid limits, and to send account, billing and security emails.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use callers' conversations to train AI models.

4. Service providers

We use these companies to run Squadron. Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

  • OpenAI generates agent answers, voice and business profiles. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default.
  • Twilio carries phone calls and creates call recordings.
  • Cloudflare runs the bridge that connects calls to the voice agent.
  • Vercel hosts the website and stores recordings and uploaded files.
  • Neon hosts our database.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Stripe processes card payments, and Mercury receives bank wires.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

5. How long we keep it

We keep business content, conversations and recordings while the account is open so the business can review them. A business can delete its account at any time from its account page. Deletion is immediate: the account, its teams, profiles, conversations, transcripts and call recordings are erased at that moment. We keep only paid invoice records, without the email address, because tax and accounting law requires them. A business can also ask us to delete a specific conversation or recording at any time.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Recordings and uploads are stored privately and are only available to the signed-in account that owns them. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected businesses without undue delay if we learn of a breach of their data.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete your account yourself from your account page. You can also ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information by emailing info@squadron.tel. If you called or chatted with a business, we will pass your request to that business and help it respond. Depending on where you live, including California and the European Union, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them. We will not treat you differently for using them.

8. Cookies

Squadron uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser remembers your setup progress while you build your team. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

Squadron is a service for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

10. Changes

We will post any update here with a new effective date, and we will email account owners about material changes.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and requests go to info@squadron.tel.
