Questions

Questions before
you recruit?

Does the agent say it is an AI? +

Yes. Every Squadron agent identifies itself as an AI at the start of every conversation, and every call begins with an audible recording notice. Squadron answers inbound conversations only; it never places outbound AI calls.

What if the agent doesn't know the answer? +

It says so, and it escalates. Every agent answers only from your Business Profile, and when a question falls outside it the agent tells the customer it does not have that information, takes a message or transfers to your on-call contact, and logs the gap in Squadron HQ so you can approve an answer for next time.

What does Squadron need from me? +

A website URL, an iOS App Store URL, or your documents. Squadron builds a Business Profile from them, shows every field with its source, and asks you to correct anything before your team goes live.

Do I need a new phone number? +

Phone lines are opening soon. When they do, Squadron will give you a number that you can publish directly or forward your existing business line to. Web chat works today with one line of code on your site.

Is there a free trial? +

No. Squadron is prepaid instead: you pay for 30 days, and there is no contract, so you can simply not renew. Plans start at $39.

How is pricing calculated? +

Each plan is a flat prepaid price for 30 days, with a set number of AI voice minutes and unlimited web chat. Extra voice minutes cost 25¢ each, prepaid in blocks of 100. Squadron never bills after the fact: when a period's prepaid amount is used, your team pauses and we email you a top-up invoice. There is no automatic upgrade and no per-agent fee.

Can my customers talk to a person instead? +

Yes, and you decide how. Choose AI first with a person on request, let every customer choose between a person and the AI team, put a person first with the AI as your receptionist, or run AI only. The chat always shows a Talk to a person button unless you choose AI only, and every request is emailed to you and logged in Squadron HQ.

How do I cancel? +

There is nothing to cancel. Squadron has no contract and nothing renews automatically, so if you do not pay for another 30 days, your team stops at the end of the current period. You can also delete your account from your account page in one step.

Can I test my team before customers see it? +

Yes. Test it by chat, by voice in your browser, and by calling a fifteen-minute demo number from any phone. Every answer shows its source, and nothing goes live until you switch it on.

How do I add the chat to my website? +

Turn chat on in the Deploy screen and paste one line of code before the closing body tag, or into your site builder's custom code area on Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace or Wix.

How do I reach a person at Squadron? +

Ask Ace in the chat, open the Help Center, or send your question with the form at squadron.tel/help. A person replies by email, and every request gets a reference number.
