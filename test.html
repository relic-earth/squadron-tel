Step 5

Test your team.

Ask what your customers ask. Every answer shows the source it came from, and anything your profile does not cover is refused honestly and added to your knowledge queue.

Press Start to talk to your team. Your browser will ask for the microphone.
The team speaks with the Front Desk voice for the whole call and says out loud when it hands off. Messages, transfer requests and unanswered questions are recorded when you end the call.
Get a real phone number for fifteen minutes and call your team from any phone.
The call is answered by your Front Desk agent. It says it is an AI and that the call is recorded, answers from your profile, takes messages, and the transcript and recording appear in Squadron HQ as a test call.
